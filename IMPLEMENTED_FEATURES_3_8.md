# Novelty Library 3.8

- Threaded community comments with replies and comment likes.
- Follower/following connection lists on profiles.
- Independent privacy controls for followers and following lists.
- Every account automatically follows @noveltylibrary; existing accounts are backfilled.
- Stories are visible only to the story owner and their followers.
- Stories support likes and reposting to the current user's own 24-hour story rail.
- Story media bucket is private and served with signed URLs.
- Trust Score progress track is centered under the score.
