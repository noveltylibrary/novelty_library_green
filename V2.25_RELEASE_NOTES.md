# Novelty Library v2.25

## Profile page
- **Side column order** is now: Stories and Highlights, Edit profile, Accounts & Settings.
- **Accounts & Settings is its own page** (`/account`), opened from the profile page. It keeps the same sections
  (Account, Settings, Documentation, Security) and tabs. Each tab has its own address (`/account/username`,
  `/account/appearance`, `/account/notifications`, `/account/content`, `/account/cookies`, `/account/password`,
  `/account/grievances`, `/account/delete`), so Back and refresh land on the tab you were on.
  - Phone: the list, then one tab at a time with a Back link.
  - PC: the list stays on the left and the open tab shows on the right.
- **Edit profile** sections sit two beside each other on PC, like on a phone.
- **Profile card download tile** shows only the download icon and the size text (for example "4:5 PNG"); the word "Download" is gone.

## Author card
- Fixed the crash when opening an author from a review: the follower count is not known for a moment while it loads, and the
  Followers bar tried to format it. It now shows a dash until the number arrives.
- The Followers bar on the author card is purple and gold only.
- The "Download card (PNG)" button is removed from author cards (phone and PC).

## Menu
- Added a red **Sign out** at the bottom of the phone menu (signed-in readers only).
