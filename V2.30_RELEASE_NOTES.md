# Novelty Library v2.30

## Explore page: two tabs
- **About** (left) and **Discovery ⚡** (right, opens by default) sit in a tab bar at the top of Explore. Arrow keys, Home and End move between the tabs.
- **Addresses:** `/explore` opens Discovery, `/explore/about` opens About, and the old `/about` link still works and opens About (so `/about#roadmap` and any shared links keep landing where they did). The header and footer "Explore" links now point to `/explore`.
- Switching tabs replaces the history entry, so Back leaves Explore instead of bouncing between the two tabs.

## Discovery tab (new)
Card order: **Live Rankings → Analytics → Ad Block 1 → Taste Match SE → Ad Block 2** (the last ad sits at the bottom of the scroll, just above the footer).
- **Live Rankings** card: community-driven scoring (NL and R/W) and the leaderboards, with an "Open Live Rankings" jump link.
- **Analytics** card: Trust Score and review metrics, with an "Open Analytics" jump link.
- **Taste Match SE** card: vector matching, mood pills and verdict filters, a three-step explainer, a live interactive preview (up to three moods, optional verdicts, a taste fingerprint that reshapes as you choose) and an "Explore Taste Match SE" jump link. The preview uses no database; real matches stay on the Taste Match SE page.
- Bespoke line-art SVG icons (podium, trust gauge, bolt, book) with small CSS animations. All motion is switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Light and dark themes supported.

## About tab
- Keeps the manifesto, About Novelty Library, Why share your review, How it works, How we rate, the roadmap, FAQ and the call to action, in the same order with the same ad slots.
- The **Taste Match SE explainer block moved out** of About into Discovery. Everything else on About is unchanged.
