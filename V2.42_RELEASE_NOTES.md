# Novelty Library 2.42 — AdSense content-safety update

Frontend-only update. No database, Supabase, API, edge function, or backend changes.

- Restricted AdSense to individual published review pages with at least 600 characters of loaded review text.
- Added a homepage community shelf that displays up to three real published reviews with book details, excerpts, reviewer attribution, ratings, and links to the full review.
- Made the shared ad slot fail closed unless `contentReady` is explicitly enabled.
- Removed ads from the homepage hero-only screen, Discovery/Taste Match explainer, Community Reviews listing, About sections, Live Rankings side rails, comments, reader feedback lists, and collapsible Review Guidelines sections.
- Restricted Google-injected/Auto Ads on every route except individual review pages.
- Existing cookie-consent behavior is preserved.

This reduces risky placements but does not guarantee AdSense approval; Google evaluates the live site's overall original content and implementation.
